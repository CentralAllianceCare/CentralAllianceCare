Platform Features

Everything You Need in One Place

Powerful tools designed to make healthcare staffing effortless for everyone.

Smart Booking System

Interactive calendar with real-time availability. Workers set when they're free, companies post shifts — matching happens automatically.

Two-Way Ratings

Workers rate companies. Companies rate workers. Build trust with transparent reviews visible to the entire community.

Verified Credentials

Upload and verify qualifications, police checks, NDIS Worker Screening, vaccinations, and professional registrations.

In-App Messaging

Communicate directly with workers or companies. Discuss shift details, ask questions, and coordinate seamlessly.

Personalised Dashboard

Track upcoming shifts, manage bookings, view earnings, and monitor your activity all from one central hub.

Smart Notifications

Get alerted for new matching shifts, booking confirmations, messages, and rating updates in real time.
